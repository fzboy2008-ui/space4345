                  11 — SUMMARY16 / 17
                  THE BIG PICTURE

                  Key Points

                  The human body works as one connected system. Each part has a role in keeping us alive, active and aware.

                  01

                  Support

                  The skeleton gives the body shape, protects organs and works with muscles.

                  02

                  Control

                  The brain and nerves process information and coordinate body responses.

                  03

                  Circulation

                  The heart and blood vessels transport oxygen and nutrients.

                  04

                  Breathing

                  The lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide with the blood.

                  05

                  Nutrition

                  The digestive system breaks food down and absorbs nutrients.

                  06

                  Perception

                  Five sensory organs help the brain interpret the world around us.
