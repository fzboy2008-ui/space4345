      CHAPTER 15 — RECAP

      What makes us
      human.

      The body is a connected system. Each structure has a role, and every role contributes to keeping us alive, aware and in motion.

      01Structure

      The skeleton supports the body, protects organs and provides a framework for movement.

      02Control

      The brain and nervous system receive information, process it and coordinate responses.

      03Circulation

      The heart and blood vessels transport oxygen, nutrients and other essential materials.

      04Energy

      The lungs, digestive system and cells work together to supply what the body needs.

      05Protection

      Skin, bones and immune defences help protect the body from injury and infection.

      06Perception

      Five senses detect changes around us and help the brain interpret the world.
